What we do locally

Clarify. Align. Amplify.

Clarify — brand audits and brand strategy: what your business really stands for, who it is for, and why customers should choose you over the alternatives nearby.

Align — verbal and visual identity: one clear message and look across your website, Google profile, LinkedIn, brochures and shopfront.

Amplify — getting found: how your brand shows up in Google, Google Maps and AI answers such as ChatGPT and Gemini, plus social content and trade-show materials.

Areas we work with

Kingston, south-west London and beyond.

Kingston upon Thames, Surbiton, New Malden, Norbiton, Tolworth, Richmond, Twickenham, Wimbledon and the rest of Greater London. Projects across the UK and Korea run online.

New Malden has one of the largest Korean communities in Europe. HALO works in English and Korean, so we are a natural fit for Korean-owned businesses in the area and for Korean companies entering the UK.
