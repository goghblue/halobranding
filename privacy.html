Privacy

Privacy information.

HALO Branding is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

Who we are

HALO Branding is a trading name of Gina Ha, based in Kingston upon Thames, United Kingdom. Gina Ha is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. For privacy enquiries, contact gina.ha@halogbs.com.

Information you provide

When you contact us by email, you may provide your name, email address, company details and information about your enquiry. We use that information to respond to you and to discuss services you request.

Contact form

When you send an enquiry through the contact form, your name, email address, topic and message are delivered to HALO by email via the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). We use this information only to respond to your enquiry. You can also email us directly instead of using the form.

Why we use it

We process enquiry information because it is in our legitimate interests to reply to people who contact us, and, where you ask about our services, to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we go on to work together, in which case project records are kept for as long as needed for the work and for legal and accounting purposes.

Cookies and analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit this website, which pages they view and which sites referred them. It does not use cookies, does not store data on your device and does not track individual visitors across sites. The data is aggregated and processed by Cloudflare, Inc. on our behalf.

With your permission only, we also use Google Analytics (Google LLC), which sets cookies (named _ga) to measure visits, the pages viewed and whether the contact form was sent. Google Analytics does not load until you choose “Accept”. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer; if you withdraw consent, the cookies are deleted. These cookies last up to 2 years.

Service providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. GitHub, Cloudflare, Google and FormSubmit may process data outside the UK; where they do, they rely on the safeguards permitted under UK data protection law.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your personal data, or to object to processing. Contact us using the address above to make a request. You can also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
