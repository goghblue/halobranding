What might we uncover?

Illustrative example only — not a real client finding.

What the business says“We offer great customer service.”

A claim many competitors could make. On its own, it gives customers little reason to choose this brand.

What the evidence may reveal“We consistently solve complex problems others decline.”

Repeated decisions and delivery records may support a more distinctive strength. We then check whether available customer signals suggest that strength matters — and identify what still needs testing.

The outcome is not a slogan invented in a workshop. It is a positioning direction supported by the evidence available.

07 / FAQ

Before you begin.

Is this a financial brand valuation?

No. Value Discovery explores authentic strengths, customer relevance and positioning opportunities; it does not estimate the monetary value of your brand.

Are customer interviews included?

The standard Value Discovery scope includes desk-based analysis of publicly available customer signals and competitor information, not direct customer interviews or surveys. Primary customer research can be quoted separately; the report distinguishes observed evidence, interpretation and hypotheses requiring validation.

Do we need both services?

No. Brand Clarity Review is the lighter version of Value Discovery. Choose the Review for an initial assessment and immediate priorities, or the full Discovery when you need deeper evidence and positioning direction. You can start directly with either.

What does the fee include?

Each service card above lists what is covered, at the price shown. Additional stakeholder interviews, direct customer research, surveys, fieldwork, extensive competitor studies and implementation are quoted separately. The written proposal confirms scope limits.

How long does it take?

Planning estimates are 5–7 working days for a Review and 2–3 weeks for Value Discovery, depending on how quickly materials and interview time are available. Dates are confirmed in the proposal.

Is this the same as a rebrand?

No. It comes before one. Value Discovery clarifies what is worth keeping and what to strengthen; identity and brand-experience work follows separately (Align, then Amplify).

Will my materials stay confidential?

Materials you share are used only for your engagement. Confidentiality terms can be agreed in writing before you send anything sensitive.
