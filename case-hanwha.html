Hanwha Systems / Quantum Red · 2021

Making
technology
make sense.

A thermal imaging technology story, translated into a clearer market-facing narrative and a connected suite of brand communications.

ClientHanwha Systems
Year2021
RoleBrochure design · Advertising · Identity applications
SectorThermal imaging
The ChallengeCommunicating technical differentiation

Advanced technology.
A complex story.

The source materials describe a thermal imaging engine module and a micro TRSM (Temperature Reference Source Module), presented as an integrated alternative to a separate temperature-reference setup.

The communications challenge was to make the product’s functional advantages, applications and lineup understandable without losing technical credibility.

TechnologyTechnical complexity

Multiple components and specialist terminology require a clear explanation.

AdvantagePractical benefits

Space-saving integration and ease of installation need to become visible benefits.

CommunicationConsistent assets

Identity, brochures, advertisements and product information must tell one coherent story.

Strategic InsightFrom specifications to relevance
A technical advantage becomes a brand advantage when people understand why it matters.
Strategic ShiftMake the benefit legible

From a list
of features
to a clear
advantage.

The product brochure foregrounds the relationship between the thermal engine and micro TRSM. The communication brings three practical advantages into focus: no separate black body, less space and easier installation.
