SajuGPT / Stagio · 2026

Beyond prediction.
Towards guidance.

Reframing an AI fortune-reading app as a thoughtful, personalised life-guidance brand—through positioning, verbal identity, visual identity refinement and social content strategy.

ClientSajuGPT · Stagio
Year2026
RoleBrand identity · Brand strategy · Social strategy
SectorAI fortune-reading app
The brief

SAJUGPT had technical differentiators and promotional assets. The next challenge was to establish a coherent brand: what it stands for, how it speaks, what it looks like and how those choices should translate into consistent customer experiences.

Our approachClarify · Align · Amplify

Build the meaning first.
Then connect the touchpoints.

The work connected brand definition to practical expression—rather than treating logo refinement, campaign design and social posts as separate assignments.

01 · CLARIFY

Define the promise

Mission, values, positioning, brand story and the role of the service in people's lives.

02 · ALIGN

Make it recognisable

Verbal persona, tone of voice, logo system, colour, type and coherent design rules.

03 · AMPLIFY

Design for use

Social content templates, customer touchpoints and a phased brand-experience roadmap.

Our insightFrom novelty to relevance

People may come for a prediction.
They return for a sense of direction.

The strategic opportunity was to move beyond entertaining daily fortune content towards a trusted, high-tech yet human-feeling guide for decisions about relationships, career and everyday life. This is a proposed positioning—not a measured customer-behaviour outcome.

Strategic shiftPositioning

From fortune apps to personal guidance.

The verbal strategy distinguished lightweight, database-led fortune content from deeper, high-involvement, personalised guidance. Its organising idea was High-Tech × High-Touch: specialist technology expressed through warmth, clarity and empathy.

Proposed brand role · A wise, non-judgemental guide

Strategic positioning based on the project’s category and consumer analysis.

Strategic alignmentVerbal × Visual × Experience

One brand idea.
Three connected systems.

The promise needed to remain recognisable whether a customer encountered a social post, opened the app or started a conversation.

VERBAL IDENTITY

A mentor, not an oracle

Guidance centred on understanding, agency and psychological reassurance rather than fixed predictions.

VISUAL IDENTITY

Consistent by design

Logo structure, purple-led palette, typography, contrast and reusable social templates.

BRAND EXPERIENCE

Care at each moment

Awareness → discovery → consultation → retention, supported by a three-phase BX roadmap.
