Our approachClarify · Align · Amplify

From market research
to a route to market.

A strategy for expanding sales had to answer three connected questions: where to compete, why buyers should care, and how the offer could reach them.

01

Clarify.

Examined market structure, competitor alternatives, institutional purchasing needs and DOBANO’s manufacturing capabilities.

02

Align.

Defined an opportunity between basic and premium systems; connected technical competence to the human value of dignified care.

03

Amplify.

Developed messaging, segmentation and targeting, channel priorities, a five-month action plan and distribution/rental options.

Our insightThe institutional buyer

For institutional care providers,
bathing is a service decision
— not just an equipment purchase.

For care institutions, bathing requires staff time and physical effort. Investment in the bathing experience can therefore become a meaningful service decision—not merely an equipment purchase.

Strategic shiftPositioning opportunity

The space between two extremes.

The analysis contrasted lower-cost, basic equipment with expensive imported systems. DOBANO’s strategic opening was to offer meaningful functionality at a more accessible price position.
